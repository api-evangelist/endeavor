# Endeavor / WME Group

> Endeavor Group Holdings was a global sports and entertainment company representing talent and
> owning and operating events. Silver Lake completed a $25bn take-private of Endeavor on
> 2025-03-24; UFC, WWE, IMG, On Location and PBR sit under TKO Group Holdings, and the remaining
> talent, marketing, licensing and non-scripted content businesses were rebranded WME Group in 2026.
> WME Group comprises WME (talent agency), 160over90 (marketing agency), IMG Licensing and
> Pantheon Media Group. This file was GENERATED by API Evangelist from its own catalog record and
> its own live probes — Endeavor / WME Group does not publish an llms.txt.

## What is and is not here

- Endeavor / WME Group publishes **no developer program, no API documentation, no SDK and no
  OpenAPI definition**. There is no developer portal, no `api.` or `developer.` host in DNS on
  either wmegrp.com or wme.com, and no `/.well-known/` document on any WME Group host.
- What *is* machine-readable is the corporate site's own CMS: wmegrp.com runs WordPress and exposes
  the WordPress REST API, anonymously readable for the public content collections. That is a content
  API, not a product API.
- A WordPress MCP Adapter endpoint is registered on wmegrp.com and is live, but authentication-gated.
